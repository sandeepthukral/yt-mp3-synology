How to use

  1. Fill in the fields above and save — this must match your NAS's Tailscale address and AUTH_TOKEN.
  2. Open any YouTube video (youtube.com, m.youtube.com, music.youtube.com, or a youtu.be link).
  3. Click the extension's toolbar button. The MP3 downloads automatically once the NAS finishes converting it.
